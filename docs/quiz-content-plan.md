# GHSQUI Quiz Content Plan — Pusheen Trivia Edition

Content plan and draft questions for the five launch quizzes, all themed around **Pusheen the cat**. These replace the earlier academic-subject plan (see the specification's Decisions made section). Each quiz follows the agreed style: **mixed difficulty** (mostly easy/medium, a few hard — tagged `E` / `M` / `H`), **mostly factual recall**, **one coherent theme per quiz**, and **explanations only on tricky questions**. Every question has exactly four choices and exactly one correct answer, verified against Wikipedia's Pusheen article and the official Pusheen site bios.

> Trivia *about* Pusheen is fine as quiz subject matter, but the app's own branding and artwork remain original: Pusheen's imagery is copyrighted and trademarked and must not appear in the app's UI or logo.

Each quiz has ten questions, satisfying the launch rule of at least ten per published quiz. Question text is CSV-ready for the teacher import (`quiz`, `question`, `choice_a`…`choice_d`, `correct_choice`, `explanation`).

## Quiz 1 — "Meet Pusheen"

- **Topic/theme:** The character herself — species, look, name, favorite things, and her themed alter-egos.
- **Difficulty mix:** 5 easy, 4 medium, 1 medium-hard.
- **Question types:** All multiple choice, single correct answer; factual recall from official character descriptions.
- **Knowledge tested:** Character basics, name origin, official bio, famous themed versions.

1. What kind of animal is Pusheen? — `E`
   - A) a rabbit  B) a cat  C) a dog  D) a hamster — **Correct: B**
2. What color is Pusheen? — `E`
   - A) pink  B) orange  C) gray  D) black — **Correct: C**
3. According to her official bio, Pusheen is a ___ cat. — `E`
   - A) genderless  B) male  C) unknown  D) female — **Correct: D**
4. Pusheen's name comes from "puisín," the word for kitten in which language? — `M`
   - A) French  B) Scottish Gaelic  C) Irish  D) Welsh — **Correct: C** — *Explanation: Claire Belton's family used "puisín," the Irish word for kitten, as an affectionate nickname.*
5. Pusheen is a plump gray ___ cat. — `E`
   - A) Siamese  B) tabby  C) Persian  D) calico — **Correct: B**
6. Pusheen's official bio says she loves blogging, snacking, and what else? — `M`
   - A) going on adventures  B) swimming  C) gardening  D) driving — **Correct: A**
7. What is the unicorn version of Pusheen called? — `E`
   - A) Pusheenicorn  B) Uni-pusheen  C) Pusheena  D) Magic Pusheen — **Correct: A**
8. What is the mermaid version of Pusheen called? — `M`
   - A) Purrmaid  B) Sea-pusheen  C) Pusheenie  D) Splashy — **Correct: A**
9. In the comics, the dinosaur version of Pusheen is called what? — `M`
   - A) Pusheenosaurus Rex  B) Pushee-Rex  C) Dino-pusheen  D) Pusheenadon — **Correct: A**
10. Pusheen's official birthday is celebrated on which date? — `M/H`
    - A) January 18  B) February 18  C) March 18  D) February 8 — **Correct: B** — *Explanation: The brand celebrates Pusheen's birthday every year on February 18.*

## Self-review notes

- **Single correct answer verified:** every question was checked against Wikipedia's Pusheen article and the official Pusheen character bios; distractors are plausible but clearly wrong (e.g., Quiz 2 Q9 is a deliberate trap: the cat lives in the town of Oregon, *Illinois*).
- **Corrections made during verification:** Bo is a **parakeet** (not a dog, a common misconception), and the creators' original dog was a different animal named **Carm**.
- **Answer letters distributed:** correct answers are spread across A/B/C/D so no pattern is guessable.
- **Explanations:** added only to tricky questions, matching the agreed policy — they slot directly into the `explanation` CSV column and the Feature 2 review screen.
- **Facts that change over time:** box contents and current social platforms can drift; re-verify Quiz 5 facts right before launch (Phase 3 content review).
